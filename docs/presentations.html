Before you present

Four useful checks

  1. Keep credits, source information and speaker notes with reused slides.
  2. Check facts against the source review date and ask the content or science owner about changes in meaning.
  3. Check contrast, reading order and alternative text; caption video.
  4. Check the presentation on the actual display or delivery format.

Reuse, adapt or ask?

Use released material within its usage notes. Ask the content owner about changed claims; ask Creative Production about substantial design work.
