Self-service guide

Start with a suitable template and build the talk around your audience.

Find presentation resources

Build the story

  • Give each slide a clear purpose and use readable, concise headings.
  • Use the supplied layouts and keep spacing, alignment and typography consistent.
  • Use imagery and charts to explain the message; retain their sources and credits.

Check before presenting

  • Try the deck on the intended display and check that text and charts are readable from the audience’s position.
  • Check contrast, alternative text and reading order. Caption any video.
  • Check facts, dates and any adapted claims with the appropriate content owner.
