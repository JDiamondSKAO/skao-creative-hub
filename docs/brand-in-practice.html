Practical application

Use the existing templates and artwork to keep your work recognisably SKAO.

A quick self-check

  • Logo: supplied artwork, correct variant, clear space and original proportions.
  • Type: Noto Sans, with Verdana as fallback.
  • Colour: the sourced palette and readable contrast.
  • Images: relevant to the message, with asset-specific credits and permissions.
