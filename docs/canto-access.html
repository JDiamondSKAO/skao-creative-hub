Find and use media

Canto is the SKAO’s media library. Browse and download approved photos, video and artwork, with the credit and usage notes for each one.

Open the Canto library ↗

First time in Canto?

  1. Open the Canto library and choose Register Now.
  2. Register with your SKAO email address.
  3. Once your account is active, sign in with the same details each time.

Find, check, download

  1. Search for a subject, event or location, or browse the albums.
  2. Open an asset to read its credit, usage notes and download options.
  3. Download the version you need and keep the credit with your work.
I can’t sign in or download a file

Send the page or asset link and the message you see through the Comms helpdesk. Never include your password.

I want to add material to Canto

Uploading has its own route. See how to share photos or video.
