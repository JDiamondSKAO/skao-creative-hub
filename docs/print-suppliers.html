Production handoff

Communications can recommend trusted print suppliers for your location and the kind of job you have.

Ask for a supplier recommendation ↗

  1. Ask us for a recommendation

    Tell us where you are and where the items need to go, what you need printed, its size and quantity, and your deadline and budget. We’ll suggest suppliers that suit the job.

  2. Follow the purchasing process

    Check which procurement rules apply with the responsible team, and agree who gets the quote, who approves the spend and who places the order.

  3. Agree sign-off and dates

    Decide who signs off the content and the final proof, and agree production and delivery dates with the supplier before you commit.

  4. Prepare the files

    Ask the supplier for their specification and set up your artwork to match. Prepare files for print walks through it.
