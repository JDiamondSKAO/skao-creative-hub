    Before you start

    Looking for something specific?
    • Hero images: search by subject; check the crop, resolution and use restrictions.
    • Telescopes: search by telescope, location and date; tell photography apart from artist impressions.
    • Events: search by event name and date; check the consent information for images of people.
    • B-roll: search by subject and location; check duration, format, audio and usage restrictions.
    • Recordings: search by event, speaker and date; check permission for the audience and any excerpt.
    • Animations: search by subject; check the science and technical depiction are still current.
    Before using an asset
    • Read the credit, copyright and usage information on the asset.
    • Check that it suits your intended audience and use. Availability alone is not permission for every use.
    • Choose an appropriate download format; keep the credit with your working file.
    Can’t find or access the material?

    Include your search terms, intended use and the link or error shown.
