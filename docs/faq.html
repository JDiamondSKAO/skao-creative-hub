Help and next steps

Short answers to the most common Hub questions.

Where are templates and slides?

Start with Templates and assets. The source pages show file availability. Presentation releases belong in the Hub.

Where are photos and video?

Use the Canto library ↗ and check each asset’s credit and permitted use.

Can I adapt material myself?

Follow the release’s usage notes and keep credits. Ask the relevant content owner about changes to facts or meaning; ask Creative Production about substantial design work.

Who approves the work?

Identify the appropriate content owner and final reviewer in the brief. The approval route depends on the work and should be confirmed rather than assumed.

Can I get an urgent turnaround?

Explain the use date and any flexibility. Scope and timing need agreement.

Still stuck?

Include the relevant page or asset link.
