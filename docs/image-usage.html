Credits and permitted use

Read the information on the individual asset before reusing it.

Check the asset in Canto ↗

Check these details

  • Credit: copy the required wording from the asset record; do not guess a generic SKAO or partner credit.
  • Rights: check the intended use, audience, restrictions and any consent information.
  • Format: use a suitable download, not a screenshot or watermarked preview.
  • Meaning: retain context and identify artist impressions or visualisations appropriately.
