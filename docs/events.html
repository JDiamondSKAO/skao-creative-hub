Event organisation

Confirm with the responsible teams
  • Venue and supplier specifications, dimensions and delivery requirements.
  • Content owner, proof reviewer and budget approval.
  • Supplier lead time and the final artwork deadline.
  • Applicable safety requirements with the event organiser and HSSE.
