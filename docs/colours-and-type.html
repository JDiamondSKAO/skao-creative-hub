Accent palettes

Use accents alongside the two primary colours, which should make up most of any design. Select a colour to copy its hex value.

Science15 colours · for science-related products and material
Technology10 colours · for engineering, big data, software and technology
Sites5 colours · for the telescope sites, local heritage and communities

Apply colour carefully

  • Use navy for text and links on light backgrounds. Magenta works well as an accent; check each text/background pairing.
  • The colours are designed for screen (RGB). CMYK and Pantone equivalents print slightly duller, so check a proof with your supplier.
  • Use labels, symbols or patterns as well as colour to communicate meaning.
