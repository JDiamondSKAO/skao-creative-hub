Before supplier handoff

Every supplier and product is a little different, so set up and export your file to the supplier’s specification rather than a one-size-fits-all setting.

  1. Get the supplier’s specification first

    Ask for the finished size, the bleed and safe area, the colour profile and the PDF standard they accept. If anything is missing from the quote, ask before you export.

  2. Set up bleed and safe area

    Extend backgrounds and images past the trim by the bleed the supplier asks for, and keep text and logos inside the safe area so nothing important is trimmed off.

  3. Check colour and images

    Use the colour profile the supplier gives you and make sure images are sharp at the printed size. Spot colours, foils and other special finishes need setting up exactly as the supplier describes.

  4. Export and check the PDF

    Export to the supplier’s PDF standard with fonts embedded. Then check the PDF itself, not just your design file: text, page order, size and image quality.

  5. Get sign-off before it goes

    Make sure the content owner has approved the final artwork, and agree the proof, quantity, deadline and delivery details with the supplier.

Not sure about a setting?

Send the supplier’s specification and your draft to the Comms helpdesk.
