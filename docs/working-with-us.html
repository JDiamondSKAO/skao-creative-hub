Work with Creative Production

How we work

Start with an existing resource. If you need new work or a review, bring a clear brief.

Prepare a request

Ask the team

  • Request creative helpAlready have a brief? Send it through the creative helpdesk with your files or source links.
  • What we can help withCreative Production helps shape and produce agreed creative materials. The brief determines the work and the capacity required.
  • Agree scope and timingGive the actual use date and explain what depends on it. Delivery timing is agreed after the brief and capacity are reviewed.

Specific requests

  • Brief video or animationCheck existing footage first, then explain what a new piece needs to achieve.
  • Plan print materialsDefine what needs producing before asking for artwork or a quote.
  • Work with print suppliersConfirm the supplier, specification, budget and approvals for the actual job.
  • Request business cardsConfirm the required contact details, quantity and delivery need.
