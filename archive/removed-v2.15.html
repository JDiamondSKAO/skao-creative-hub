working-with-us.html

Get help before round 3: "What we can help with" and the earlier "What happens next" wording.

Most everyday work can start from the Hub’s templates and guides. For new work or a review, send a brief to the creative helpdesk.

Open the creative helpdesk ↗

What to include in your brief

Copy it, paste it into the helpdesk form and fill in the gaps.

Purpose
What it is, who it’s for and what it should help them do.
Timing
When it will be used, and why then.
Output
Format, size and quantity.
People
Who supplies the content, and who approves the result.
Inputs
Source files or links, budget and any other constraints.

Also include, for

Video or animation
  • Where it will be shown, and how long it can be.
  • Existing footage, a draft script, images and source information.
  • Accessibility needs such as captions.
  • Any filming, and who needs to be available for it.

Check Video and animation first: existing footage may already do the job.

Print
  • The item, its dimensions and how many you need.
  • Existing artwork or draft content, and where images come from.
  • Where it should be delivered, and the supplier’s deadline.
  • Finishing, such as lamination or folding.

Ordering from a supplier? See Work with print suppliers and Prepare files for print.

Business cards
  • Your name and job title, exactly as they should appear.
  • The contact details for the card.
  • How many, in which languages, and where to deliver them.
  • Who will approve the proof.

What happens next

  1. We replyThe team reads your brief and replies, often with a question or two.
  2. You agree the detailsWhat will be made, who reviews it and when it’s delivered.
  3. Work startsOnce that’s agreed.

Timing depends on when the content is ready, how complex the work is, how quickly reviewers respond and any supplier deadlines.

Urgent? Give the real deadline, the reason for it and what could be cut back. A date isn’t fixed until it’s agreed.

What we can help with

  • Design and layout for publications, presentations and graphics
  • Video, animation and digital work
  • Visual identity, templates and reusable resources
  • Creative materials for events, print and displays

You bring the purpose, the content and the person who approves it. Event logistics and editorial decisions stay with the teams that own them.

Before placing an order

  • Check the applicable procurement process with the responsible team.
  • Agree who obtains the quote, approves expenditure and places the order.
  • Confirm who signs off content and the final artwork proof.
  • Agree production and delivery dates with the supplier.
