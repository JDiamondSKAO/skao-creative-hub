          Plan merchandise

          merchandise.html

          Plan the work

          Start with the purpose and audience. Check existing stock before commissioning new items.

          Prepare the creative brief

          Existing designs to reuse

          • SKAO apparel, promotional items, stationery and stickers.
          • SRCNet apparel.

          Ask for the artwork before briefing something new.

          Include in the brief

          • Why the item is useful for the audience.
          • Available stock or an existing design that could be reused.
          • Quantity, budget, destination and use date.
          • Supplier requirements, material choices and the artwork reviewer.

          Plan stand graphics

          stands.html

          Plan the work

          Check the existing kit and the venue’s specification before briefing new graphics.

          Prepare the creative brief

          Include in the brief

          • Available stand system and reusable graphics.
          • Venue dimensions, supplier artwork templates and display restrictions.
          • Audience, key messages and draft content.
          • Artwork reviewer, supplier deadline and event organiser contacts.
